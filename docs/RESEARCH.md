# Rendering research for Portal Valley

The camera passes within a few metres of the ground, rocks and trees. A contact shadow under a stone, exposed roots against the bank and a visible change from gravel to wet silt contribute at precisely the distances where the original conifers and flat rock faces broke the illusion. This favors detailed assets near the path and progressively cheaper silhouettes farther up the valley.

## Three interactive references

These observations come from the linked projects and their source. Mobile behavior below describes the authors' controls or settings. No external demo received a physical-phone benchmark in this review.

| Example | Rendering, visual result and interaction | Take into the valley | Exclude and why | Rights and reach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keyboard, pointer and touch movement are documented. The authors reduce texture size and vegetation density for touch. | Place distinct plant groups along habitat boundaries. Preserve the shape of near ferns and visible trunks. Instance shared geometry and vary orientation, scale and spacing. | The showcase warns about GPU cost and potentially long construction. ## Asset preparation and rendering choices

| Technique | Decision and verification |
| --- | --- |
| Scans and artist-authored geometry | Scan-derived silhouettes and calibrated material maps belong closest to the camera. Retain a low-resolution collider independently of visual topology. For an original scan, shoot overlapping views with consistent light, solve camera poses and geometry offline, remove baked illumination, then bake a cleaned high-resolution source onto a simplified UV-mapped mesh. Keep source, author, license and derivative records together. [Poly Haven workflow](https://blog.polyhaven.com/photoscanned-texture-creation-process/) · [Texture standards](https://docs.polyhaven.com/en/technical-standards/textures) |
| PBR map roles | Mark albedo as sRGB. Keep normal, roughness and AO samples as data. Natural soil, bark and rocks use dielectric material settings. Match texture scale to metres and reserve geometric displacement for visible silhouettes. Inspect wet and dry surfaces under the same directional light. [Three.js color management](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/color-management.html) · [MeshStandardMaterial](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/MeshStandardMaterial.html) |
| Terrain and vegetation | Use a shared height function for the rendered bank and player floor. Thin grass at the waterline and along the trail. Cluster repeated plants and rocks for submission efficiency. Measure both triangles and draw calls: an instanced batch still pays for submitted instances and overlapping leaf fragments. Partition large batches when visibility tests justify the expense. [InstancedMesh](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/InstancedMesh.html) · [LOD](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/LOD.html) |
| Lighting and water | A dominant sun, diffuse sky light and local contact shadows establish direction and scale. An environment map or prefiltered HDR can supply specular detail. Test exposure against sunlit rock and shaded soil independently. A live reflection rerenders the scene; compare a restrained environment approximation with a reduced-resolution planar pass from the same viewing position. [PBR environment guidance](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/MeshStandardMaterial.html) · [Three.js Reflector](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Reflector.html) |
| GLB, Meshopt and texture delivery | Prefer glTF 2.0 mesh assets with metre units, shared materials and simplified distant variants. Inspect output, remove unused data and deduplicate before compression. Benchmark a Meshopt-encoded candidate with a configured meshopt decoder. A KTX2/Basis candidate needs a deployed transcoder and renderer capability detection. JPEG/WebP reduce transfer size but usually expand in GPU memory. Verify each candidate on a cold load, during scene disposal and on a target device. [glTF Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev/) · [GLTFLoader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFLoader.html) · [KTX2Loader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/KTX2Loader.html) |
| Asset rights | Poly Haven assigns CC0 to its distributed HDRIs, models and textures. Its website artwork, preview renders and logos are separate. ambientCG publishes CC0 asset files. Use the asset's download and provenance page, record any upstream exceptions and retain third-party notices for libraries. [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license) · [ambientCG license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) |
| Gaussian splatting | A bounded scan from a known capture volume is a candidate for a later exhibit. Spark integrates oriented splats into Three.js. A freely traversed valley still needs stable stereo viewpoints, coverage behind trees, contact geometry, editable lighting and a collision proxy. An isolated trial should use a rights-cleared capture and compare load, resident resources, temporal detail and stereo comfort against the mesh route. [Spark overview](https://sparkjs.dev/docs/overview/) · [Spark LOD](https://sparkjs.dev/docs/lod/) |
| WebGPU | Retain the pinned WebGL2 renderer for this review. Three.js's alternative renderer selects a WebGPU backend where supported and can fall back to WebGL2. That does not establish parity for this app's custom GLSL, reflectors, split view and WebXR paths. A future side-by-side experiment should carry identical assets, viewpoints, resolution and session type through both renderers. [WebGPURenderer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGPURenderer.html) |
| Tracking and XR | Transfer one decoded frame at a time to the worker. Time frame capture, model inference, result dispatch and presentation separately. Compare delegates with both scene drawing and tracking active. The WebXR path needs a secure context, capability checks, appropriate reference space and explicit entry. Test physically because controller selection, eye separation and comfort require real equipment. [Hand Landmarker](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker) · [WebXR Device API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebXR_Device_API) |
| Profiling | Log frame intervals without clipping them to the simulation timestep. Aggregate renderer counters across the entire frame, including water, shadows and both eyes. Chrome traces distinguish script, long tasks and rendering work. Spector.js can inspect individual draws. GPU timer queries require extension detection, deferred result reads and rejection of disjoint samples. Heap size, geometry count and texture count describe different resources. [Chrome Performance](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/devtools/performance/overview/) · [Spector.js](https://github.com/BabylonJS/Spector.js) · [GPU timer queries](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/EXT_disjoint_timer_query) |

The first acceptance decision belongs to the actual valley screenshots and route samples. The example projects establish technique and provenance, while the gallery's device matrix determines which quality profile can ship.
